00 Scope & Who We Are

This Privacy Policy explains how VoidVPN: Fast Secure VPN (the “Application,” “VoidVPN,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses, discloses, and protects information in connection with your use of the Application on mobile and desktop devices. It applies to everyone who downloads, installs, or uses VoidVPN, wherever they are located.

The developer and data controller responsible for VoidVPN is Aneeqa Naveed. Questions, requests, or complaints about this policy can be sent to Aneeqanaveed@icloud.com.

By downloading, installing, or using VoidVPN, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Application.

01 Information We Collect

As a VPN service, VoidVPN is built to minimize what it collects. We only collect what is necessary to provide the service, keep it secure, bill for subscriptions, and fix problems.

Account & subscription information

  • Email address, if you create an account or contact support — used for login, receipts, and responding to you.
  • Subscription status and purchase history (e.g. plan tier, renewal date). Payment card and billing details are collected and processed directly by Apple's App Store or Google Play Billing; VoidVPN does not receive or store your full payment card number.

Device & diagnostic information

  • Device model, operating system and version, app version, and language/region settings — used to keep the app compatible and to prioritize bug fixes.
  • Crash logs and performance diagnostics (e.g. a crash stack trace, memory warnings) — used only to identify and fix defects.
  • Aggregate, non-identifying network performance metrics (such as overall server load or general latency bands) used to choose which server locations to expand or maintain. These metrics are not tied to your individual browsing activity.

Support communications

  • Anything you send us directly — emails, screenshots, or descriptions of an issue — retained only as long as needed to resolve your request.

02 What We Never Log

VoidVPN operates a strict no-activity-logs policy. Regardless of what plan you're on or which server you connect to, we do not record:

  • The websites, apps, or services you visit or use while connected
  • Your DNS queries
  • The content of your traffic, or its destination IP addresses
  • Your originating (real) IP address once a session log is closed
  • Connection timestamps or session duration tied to your individual browsing activity

Some ephemeral, in-memory connection data (such as which server you're currently routed through) exists only for as long as needed to maintain your active session, and is not written to persistent logs or storage.

03 How We Use Information

We use the limited information described above to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the VPN connection and related features (kill switch, server selection, split tunneling if offered);
  • Process subscriptions and verify entitlement to paid features via the App Store or Google Play;
  • Diagnose, fix, and prevent technical issues and abuse of the service;
  • Respond to support requests you send us;
  • Comply with legal obligations; and
  • Communicate important service notices (e.g. renewal reminders, security notices). We do not use your data for third-party advertising.

06 The VPN Tunnel & Kill Switch

When connected, VoidVPN routes your device's internet traffic through an encrypted tunnel to one of our VPN servers, which then forwards it to its destination on your behalf. This masks your real IP address from the sites and services you visit, and encrypts your traffic from your device to our server so that it cannot be read by your network operator or others on the same network.

If the app offers a kill switch, enabling it blocks all internet traffic on your device if the encrypted tunnel unexpectedly drops, so that traffic never falls back to your unprotected connection without your knowledge. Server locations are chosen for performance and legal operating environment; connecting through a given location does not create a persistent record of your activity there.

11 Security

VoidVPN uses industry-standard AES-256 encryption to secure your connection between your device and our VPN servers. We apply technical and organizational safeguards — including encryption in transit, access controls limiting who can reach account and diagnostic data, and regular review of our infrastructure — to protect the information we do hold. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to keep the amount of data at risk as small as possible by not collecting what we don't need in the first place.
